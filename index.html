Hello, I'm Imdadul Haque Sourav, a biomedical engineer from BUET and a prospective PhD student for Fall 2027.

I build deep learning models for medical images that use the context a clinician would. My undergraduate thesis at the mHealth Research Group, BUET, supervised by Dr. Taufiq Hasan, feeds patients' epidemiological risk factors into a network that segments and classifies oral lesions from smartphone photos. To train and test it, I led the curation of Bangladesh's first clinical oral cancer dataset with the Shanti Cancer Foundation, Dhaka.

I am currently a remote research intern with Dr. Sayeed Shafayet Chowdhury at Indiana University Indianapolis, where I benchmark vision foundation models for predicting optic nerve head measurements from rodent retinal OCT.

I completed my B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering in July 2026 at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).
