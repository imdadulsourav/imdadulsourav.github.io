Undergraduate thesisJanuary 2025 – presentManuscript in preparation

Epidemiologically-Informed Guided Attention for Oral Cavity Lesion Classification from Smartphone Imagery

Oral cancer is often caught late where specialist screening is scarce. This work asks whether a phone photo, paired with what a clinician already knows about the patient, is enough to flag suspicious lesions. A multitask network segments and classifies the lesion with two heads, Guided Attention Pooling focuses its features on the lesion, and a new Epidemiological Prior Layer brings patient risk factors into the network alongside the image.

Supervisor: Dr. Taufiq Hasan, Professor, BME, BUET. The abstract was accepted as an ePoster at the World Cancer Congress 2026, Hong Kong.

  1. Dataset500 smartphone images from 130 patients, curated with the Shanti Cancer Foundation, Dhaka
  2. Multitask networkDual heads segment the lesion and classify it
  3. Guided attentionGuided Attention Pooling weights features inside the lesion
  4. Epidemiological priorPatient risk factors join the visual features before the decision

Consensus-Driven Label Cleaning for Robust Fundus Disease Classification

An independent project. Several models vote on each label of a noisy clinical fundus dataset, and images they agree are mislabelled are removed before training. Two feature-level losses, a Frequency-Adaptive Anchor Loss for rare classes and a Confusion-Aware Margin Loss for clinically confusable pairs, tackle class imbalance and inter-class confusion together.

Retinal imagingJuly 2025

Impact of Image Processing Techniques on Retinal Lesion Classification

Five deep learning architectures trained on more than 32,000 OCT scans across four retinal conditions. A preprocessing pipeline that strips invalid image borders and applies CLAHE contrast enhancement raised accuracy by up to 4.75%. PI: Samiul Based Shuvo, Assistant Professor, BME, BUET.
